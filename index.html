Laboratory of Plant Immunity & Molecular Genetics

Engineering Durable Nonhost Resistance & Translational Crop Immunity

Led by Dr. Binod Bihari Sahu at the Department of Life Science, NIT Rourkela, our group deciphers the molecular architecture of nonhost resistance (NHR), fungal effector biology, one-carbon metabolic defense, and iron biofortification across Arabidopsis, Rice (Oryza sativa), and Soybean (Glycine max).

Academic Biography & Career Trajectory

Dr. Binod Bihari Sahu is an Associate Professor in the Department of Life Science at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Rourkela, Odisha, India. Before joining NIT Rourkela in February 2014, he conducted five years of postdoctoral research (2009–2014) at the Department of Agronomy, Iowa State University (USA) in Prof. Madan K. Bhattacharyya's laboratory, where he pioneered forward-genetics identification of Arabidopsis Nonhost Resistance (NHR) genes (including US-patented PSS1 and PSS30) and engineered them into transgenic soybean against Phytophthora sojae and Fusarium virguliforme.

He earned his Ph.D. in Biotechnology from the BRIC-Institute of Life Sciences (ILS), Bhubaneswar (Utkal University) under Dr. Birendra Prasad Shaw, elucidating the biochemical and molecular regulation of plasma membrane H+-ATPase and salt-induced transcriptomes in the halophyte Suaeda maritima and rice.

Scientific Investigations

Core Research Thrusts

Bridging molecular genetics, effectoromics, metabolomics, and crop biotechnology to safeguard global rice and legume production.

Pillar 01

Nonhost Resistance (NHR) & Translational Immunity

Characterizing Arabidopsis thaliana NHR genes (PSS1, PSS30, IMPORTIN ALPHA 2) and transferring functional orthologous cassettes into rice and soybean to confer durable, broad-spectrum resistance against hemibiotrophic and necrotrophic pathogens.

ArabidopsisTransgenic RicePSS Genes
Pillar 02

Rice Blast & Sheath Blight Effector Biology

Dissecting the infection strategies of Magnaporthe oryzae (Rice Blast) and Rhizoctonia solani (Sheath Blight). Developing effector- and metabolite-based plant vaccination strategies and profiling host programmed cell death (PCD).

M. oryzaeR. solaniPlant Vaccines
Pillar 03

One-Carbon (1C) Metabolism & Defense Metabolomics

Investigating how AtMTHFR2-regulated one-carbon metabolism, folate/methionine cycles, and secondary metabolite reprogramming orchestrate broad-spectrum systemic immunity against fungal invasion.

AtMTHFR21C MetabolismLC-MS Metabolomics
Pillar 04

Rhizosphere Microbiome & Abiotic-Biotic Cross-Tolerance

Harnessing beneficial rhizobacteria (such as Exiguobacterium indicum OMCW-10) for simultaneous hexavalent chromium Cr(VI) phytoremediation and biocontrol of rice blast, alongside halophyte salt-stress genomics.

BioprimingRhizosphere OmicsSalt Stress
Pillar 05

Rice Panicle Architecture & Iron Biofortification

Decoding ethylene/auxin crosstalk and miRNA networks governing spikelet grain filling in compact vs. lax rice panicles, alongside genetic biofortification to elevate bioavailable Iron (Fe) in polished rice grain.

Grain FillingFe BiofortificationPanicle Plasticity
Pillar 06

Computational Genomics & Structural Interactomics

Applying dual RNA-seq transcriptomics, sequence-based polymorphic (SBP) marker mapping, and in-silico host-pathogen protein docking (ChimeraX / AlphaFold) to accelerate candidate resistance gene discovery.

Dual RNA-SeqSBP Markers3D Interactomics
